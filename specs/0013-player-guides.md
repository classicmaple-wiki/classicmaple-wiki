# 0013: Player guides

Status: draft, waiting for owner approval

## Problem

Players are already making good guides for Classic World on YouTube and elsewhere. The wiki has no place to point at them. The owner wants a Player guides section: a list of community guides, each with a short summary in our words, the creator's name, and a link to the original. The first entry is ProfessorX Maple's video on reaching one million mesos, https://youtu.be/m-DN4kBH_nY.

## Goals and non-goals

Goals:

- A Player guides section in the sidebar with an overview page that lists every player guide as a card.
- One page per player guide: the creator's credit and link at the top, then a summary in our own words that links to the wiki's own quest, monster, map, and item pages.
- A way for players to suggest a guide without GitHub.
- The first entry, the meso guide, live with the section.

Non-goals:

- Embedding videos. An embedded player sets Google cookies, and the privacy page promises no tracking. We link.
- Copying a creator's text, thumbnails, or timestamps. We write our own summary and credit them.
- Prices. Meso prices change daily and are not wiki data. A guide may link the creator's pricing tool.
- Raising any value to verified. A video is a rank 3 source in `docs/data-research.md`: leads only, `unverified`.
- Accounts, ratings, or comments. Those belong to the community platform (backlog P4c).

## Requirements

- [ ] `/player-guides/` lists every player guide as a card with title, creator, platform, level range when known, and build.
- [ ] Each guide page shows a credit box at the top: "A guide by {creator} on {platform}", a link to the original, the date we watched or read it, and a note that the wiki summarized it and that values are unverified.
- [ ] The guide body links to at least one wiki page for every quest, monster, map, or item it names.
- [ ] The sidebar shows Player guides after Economy and systems.
- [ ] How to contribute has a short "Suggest a player guide" paragraph that points at the correction form.
- [ ] A GitHub issue form, Player guide, for people who use GitHub.
- [ ] The first guide, Making your first million mesos, is live, credited to ProfessorX Maple, and links to the Cursed Doll, Jane, and Icarus quest pages.
- [ ] Every value from a video is written as the creator's report, never as a wiki fact, and the page's status reads unverified.
- [ ] `npm run validate` and `npm run build` pass with no broken internal links.

## Design

Player guides are words, so they live in `src/content/docs/player-guides/`, one MDX file per guide, with the standard page layout, search, and sidebar. No new data folder.

Frontmatter, added to `pageFrontmatter` in `schemas/index.ts` as optional fields:

```yaml
title: Making your first million mesos
description: Three quest lines that pay in scrolls, from a video by ProfessorX Maple.
creator: ProfessorX Maple
creator_url: https://www.youtube.com/@ProfessorXMaple
guide_url: https://youtu.be/m-DN4kBH_nY
guide_platform: YouTube
watched_on: 2026-10-19
level_range: [36, 46]
build: founders-2026-10-06
```

Files:

- `schemas/index.ts`: the five new optional fields. `build` is checked against `data/builds.yaml` like every other build id. `level_range` already exists.
- `src/components/PlayerGuideCredit.astro`: the credit box. It reads the page frontmatter and renders the creator, the link with `rel="noopener"`, the platform, the date, and the unverified note. Imported at the top of each guide page.
- `src/components/PlayerGuideList.astro`: reads the docs collection, keeps entries under `player-guides/` with a `guide_url`, and renders them as cards in the same style as the place cards from spec 0007.
- `src/content/docs/player-guides/index.mdx`: what the section is, the rules in two sentences, the card list, and how to suggest one.
- `src/content/docs/player-guides/making-your-first-million-mesos.mdx`: the first guide.
- `astro.config.mjs`: a Player guides sidebar group, autogenerated from the folder, after Economy and systems.
- `src/content/docs/contributing.mdx`: a "Suggest a player guide" paragraph.
- `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/player-guide.yml`: link, creator, what it covers, build.
- `docs/data-research.md`: one line under rank 3 saying player videos and written guides are rank 3 and feed Player guides pages.

The first guide, in our own words, covers: the creator's four fundamentals (take less damage, save MP, sell equipment drops, buy defense), then the three quest lines with links to `collecting-50-cursed-dolls` and its chain, `janes-first-challenge` through `janes-final-challenge`, and `icarus-and-the-balloon` and `icaruss-hang-glider`, each with the reward the creator reports and the chance he reports, written as his report. Prices are left out. The pricing tool he uses is named once as a link. Footnotes cite the video with timestamps.

Rules for every player guide, written on the overview page:

1. Link, never embed.
2. Our words, their credit. Name the creator and link the original.
3. Rank 3 source: everything is the creator's report and stays unverified until a screenshot confirms it.
4. Classic World only. No private server guides, no old Global MapleStory guides.
5. A creator who wants their guide removed or credited differently uses the removal request form, like any other credit.
6. We do not contact creators (owner's rule). A public link with credit is fair use of a public video.
