# Sprite sources

Date: 2026-10-13. Evidence behind the first statuses in `data/sprite-sources.yaml`. Rules: `docs/policies/images.md`. Decision: 0005.

The question for each site: do its own terms forbid or restrict taking a plain game sprite from it, does it ask for credit, and is it tied to a private server. The underlying art is Nexon's in every case. Every quote below was reloaded from its page and confirmed word for word by a second check. The fetch tool cuts long passages, so some quotes are fragments.

## maplestory.io: allowed

A developer API that serves MapleStory data and sprites for official Nexon regions. https://maplestory.io/

> All aforementioned media, icons, descriptions, and character information provided by this service thereof

> are the sole property of NEXON and therefor applies to their Terms of Service.

> This service is provided out of love towards the Maplestory community and with a focus

It claims no rights of its own, sets no rule against reuse, and asks for no credit. No terms page was found. Its code repository has no license file, which covers code only. Use single mob, NPC, or item frames. Never its character renders or composites, which it builds itself.

## MapleStory Wiki (maplestorywiki.net): allowed with credit

The main MapleStory fan wiki, founded 2005.

> Except where otherwise specified, the article revisions on the MapleStory Wiki on and after 25 February 2024

> are licensed under Creative Commons BY-NC-SA 3.0.

Source: https://maplestorywiki.net/w/MapleStory_Wiki:Copyrights

> This file is copyrighted.

> assert that this qualifies as fair use of the material under United States copyright law.

Source: https://maplestorywiki.net/w/Template:Fairuse

> Images that are not directly extracted from the files must have a specified source.

Source: https://maplestorywiki.net/w/MapleStory_Wiki:Policy

Its license covers article text. Game sprites are marked as copyrighted and shown under fair use, so the wiki does not claim them. Nothing loaded forbids copying an image. Credit is a courtesy. Never take its text, which is non-commercial share alike, or any image it made itself.

## NiaMeowDB, rebranding to NiaDB (meowdb.com): allowed with credit

A Classic World fan database.

> Game names, text, sprites, and art: property of their publishers. We cannot license them to you.

> Credit NiaMeowDB (meowdb.com) and link to the specific page you used.

> Copying a whole page, catalog, or database: ask first.

> Mirroring or bulk-scraping the Site for redistribution

Source: https://meowdb.com/reuse

> You may not copy, reproduce, or redistribute them without permission.

> Use automated scripts, bots, or scrapers to access or collect data from the Site without permission.

Source: https://meowdb.com/terms

> Primary source for item, equip, and monster sprites (GMS v83).

> Contributed images used across the site.

Source: https://meowdb.com/msclassic/credits

It says sprites belong to their publishers and asks for credit with a link. The "may not copy" line in its terms is read here as covering the site's own name, logo, emojis, text, and design, which is how the surrounding terms frame it. That reading is an interpretation, so take only plain game sprites, one at a time, by hand, and never its contributed images or custom art. Its credits page names its own sprite source, so prefer that source directly. Note that its sprites come from GMS v83, an older version, which may differ from Classic World.

## Fandom (maplestory.fandom.com): ask first

Every Fandom page, and every archive copy tried, was blocked to the automated check. Nothing could be read or quoted. Someone must read the Fandom licensing and terms pages in a browser and record them here before any use.

## Hidden Street (hidden-street.net): ask first

Every page returned HTTP 403 to the automated check. A third party listing confirms it covers official regions:

> MapleStory guides and online database for Global, South East Asia and Europe versions.

Source: https://website.informer.com/hidden-street.net

Its own terms are unread. Read them in a browser before any use.

## MapleLegends and MapleRoyals: blocked

Private servers. Nexon's terms forbid posting information on private servers, and Nexon's September 2026 notice calls them infringement. No research needed. See `docs/research/assets.md`.

## Caution for all sources

A sprite from a fan site may come from an older game version and may not match Classic World. A sprite identifies an entry. It is never evidence for a stat or a fact.
