Citizenship is a new system made for Classic World. Characters at level 12 and up can use it. You get to know the people who live on Victoria Island ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)). Inkwell named it as content created specifically for Classic World ([interview](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45681)).

## How to become a citizen

The release notes name two towns ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)):

- **Henesys:** talk to [Arthur](/npcs/arthur/) in Henesys Town Hall.
- **Kerning City:** talk to [Roxy](/npcs/roxy/) in Kerning City Civic Center.

Nexon's October 15 notice changes the Kerning City map so the Civic Center is easier to reach ([October 15 notice](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/maintenance/46523)).

## What citizens get

Official residents of Henesys and Kerning City get these perks ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)):

- Special items from the town's specialty shop
- Lower cab prices
- Lower Storage Keeper prices
- Lower prices on some general store items

## Grades and Contribution

Each town has a Community Board with requests from people who live there. Finishing requests earns Contribution toward your Citizenship grade. A higher grade opens more ways to help the town ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)).
