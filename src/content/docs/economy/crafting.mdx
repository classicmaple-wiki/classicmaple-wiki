Crafting is a new system made for Classic World. Characters at level 10 and up can use it. You turn materials you collect into items for your adventure ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)). Nexon says you can make items for yourself or to share with friends ([Explore Classic World](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/explore-maplestory)).

## How it works

- Visit a crafting NPC to see what you can make and which materials you need ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)).
- The more you craft, the more your crafting skill grows. A higher skill opens more recipes ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)).
- Crafting gives crafting EXP. Nexon's October 15 notice fixes a bug where some Woodcrafting items gave EXP with a Woodcrafting Kit when they should not ([October 15 notice](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/maintenance/46523)).
