This video is about one upgrade: putting 100% scrolls on your weapon. The creator's case is that a 100% scroll always works, so the extra damage is guaranteed and nothing is lost[^1].

## Where to buy them

The creator buys 100% scrolls from an NPC vendor rather than from other players. They report a vendor in [Ellinia](/places/ellinia/), at the top left of town up the tree, that sells a 100% scroll for every weapon type[^2]. The wiki's shop pages do not list this vendor yet.

## 100% or 60%

The creator reports that a two-handed weapon's scroll costs twice as much as a one-handed one[^3]. They prefer 100% scrolls because a 60% scroll can fail, and the mesos spent on a failed scroll are gone. Their exception: for less popular classes, 60% scrolls from other players can be cheap enough to be worth the risk[^3].

## When to scroll

Do not scroll your first weapons. The creator suggests waiting until about level 35 to 40, so the weapon you scroll is one you keep for a long time before replacing it[^4].

## Why it pays

More attack lets you kill monsters in fewer hits. The creator calls the thresholds break points: going from three hits to two, or from two to one, speeds up both EXP and meso farming, so the scrolls pay for themselves[^5].

## Capes and earrings

The video ends with 100% scrolls for capes and earrings. They are cheaper, and the creator notes that scrolled earrings trade well because every class can wear them. The creator ranks both below the weapon[^6].
