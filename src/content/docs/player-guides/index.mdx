Players make good guides. This section points at them. Each page here is our short summary of a player's video or written guide, in our own words, with the creator's name and a link to the original. The summary links to the wiki's own quest, monster, map, and item pages, so you can check the details as you go.

<PlayerGuideList />

## How this section works

1. **We link, we never embed.** The original stays where the creator put it. An embedded video would set cookies, and this site sets none.
2. **Our words, their credit.** We never copy a creator's text, thumbnails, or art. Every page names the creator and links the original.
3. **It is their report.** A video or written guide is a lead, not a verified fact. Every page here is marked unverified until a screenshot from the game confirms a value. See [How to contribute](/contributing/).
4. **Classic World only.** Guides for old Global MapleStory or for private servers are not listed.
5. **Creators decide.** If you made a guide listed here and want it removed or credited differently, open a [removal request](/legal/#removal-requests). We act within a few days.
