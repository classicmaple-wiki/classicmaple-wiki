ProfessorX Maple's video is for free-to-play players who want their first million mesos without buying anything. The plan has two halves: spend less while you train, then run three quest lines whose rewards sell well.

## Four habits that save mesos

The video opens with four fundamentals[^1]:

- **Take fewer hits.** Potions are the biggest drain on a new character. Fight monsters you can hit without being hit back.
- **Save MP.** Use plain attacks when a skill would only speed things up a little.
- **Sell equipment drops.** Equipment that drops while you train can be worth more than everything else you pick up. The creator checks prices on a player-run price site, [mao.watch](https://mao.watch), and points out that a drop with a bonus stat, such as extra attack, sells for more than a plain one[^2].
- **Buy defense.** Armor with more defense means fewer potions over hundreds of fights, so it pays for itself.

## Three quest lines that pay

The creator's main tip is that some quests hand out scrolls and capes that players pay a lot for. The levels and chances below are what the video reports[^3].

### Cursed dolls, level 36

[Rowen the Fairy](/npcs/rowen-the-fairy/) in [Ellinia](/places/ellinia/) asks for cursed dolls, in a chain that starts with [Collecting 50 Cursed Dolls](/quests/collecting-50-cursed-dolls/) and ends with [Collecting 200 Cursed Dolls](/quests/collecting-200-cursed-dolls/). The chain opens after [Returned Secret Book](/quests/returned-secret-book/), the sauna quest line from [Mr. Wetbottom](/npcs/mr-wetbottom/).

The creator reports that the reward is one of two glove scrolls, a 50/50 roll between a Glove for Attack 60% scroll and a Glove for Magic Attack 60% scroll, and that the attack scroll sells for far more[^3].

### Jane's challenges, level 44

[Jane](/npcs/jane/) in [Lith Harbor](/maps/lith-harbor/) gives a line that starts with [Jane and the Mushroom](/quests/jane-and-the-mushroom/) and runs through [Jane's First Challenge](/quests/janes-first-challenge/), [Jane's Second Challenge](/quests/janes-second-challenge/), and [Jane's Final Challenge](/quests/janes-final-challenge/).

The creator reports a one in four chance at one of four glove scrolls at the end, with prices from modest to high depending on which one lands[^4].

### Icarus capes, level 46

[Icarus](/npcs/icarus/) in [Kerning City](/places/kerning-city/) has a line that runs [Icarus's Hang Glider](/quests/icaruss-hang-glider/) and [Icarus and the Balloon](/quests/icarus-and-the-balloon/).

The creator reports that it ends with one of three Icarus capes, each of which sells for a good sum[^5].

## Two more tips

- **Train where the drops sell.** Pick maps whose monsters drop equipment people want, so a training session pays twice: EXP and items to sell[^6].
- **Park mesos in cash items.** Once the mesos pile up, the creator holds some value in cash shop items, which he expects to keep their worth. That is his view, not advice from this wiki[^7].
