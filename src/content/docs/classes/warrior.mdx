At level 10, talk to [Dances with Balrog](/npcs/dances-with-balrog/) in [Perion](/places/perion/) to become a Warrior ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)). At level 30, talk to Dances with Balrog again and pick Fighter, Page, or Spearman ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)). Nexon's page calls the first job Swordsman ([Warrior page](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/classes-and-jobs/46083/warrior)).

The official notes name no requirement other than the level. If the game asks for more, please add it with a screenshot.

Founder's Access has first and second job only ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)). Third job was open for testing at level 70 in Closed Online Test 2, and Nexon said test content may change ([test 2 notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/43432/closed-online-test-2-release-notes)). Nexon has not said when third job comes to the live game. Inkwell said content such as third job may arrive in stages ([interview](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45681)).

## Stats

</Fragment>

STR is the Warrior's main stat. It raises Warrior damage and Weapon Def. DEX raises Warrior damage a little and raises Accuracy ([Character Stats guide](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/maple-guides/all/46103/character-stats)).

You get 5 AP each level. From level 10 you get 3 SP each level, plus 1 extra SP when you take first and second job ([Character Stats guide](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/maple-guides/all/46103/character-stats)).

## Skills by job

</Fragment>

Nexon's [Warrior page](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/classes-and-jobs/46083/warrior) lists these skills. It also explains what each one does.

- **Swordsman (first job):** Improved HP Recovery, Max HP Increase, Precise Strikes, Iron Body, Power Strike, Slash Blast.
- **Fighter (second job):** Sword Mastery, Axe Mastery, Final Attack: Sword, Final Attack: Axe, Sword Booster, Axe Booster, Rage, Rush.
- **Page (second job):** Sword Mastery, Blunt Weapon Mastery, Final Attack: Sword, Final Attack: Blunt Weapon, Sword Booster, Blunt Weapon Booster, Threaten, Rush.
- **Spearman (second job):** Spear Mastery, Polearm Mastery, Final Attack: Spear, Final Attack: Polearm, Spear Booster, Polearm Booster, Iron Will, Rush.

All three second jobs list Rush. Fighter and Page share some skill names, but the page gives them different effects. Fighter's Final Attack: Sword hits up to 3 enemies, and Page's hits one ([Warrior page](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/classes-and-jobs/46083/warrior)).

Skill levels, costs, and damage numbers are not in the official pages yet. If you have them from the game, please add them with a screenshot.
