At level 10, talk to [Dances with Balrog](/npcs/dances-with-balrog/) in [Perion](/places/perion/) to become a Warrior[^1]. At level 30, talk to Dances with Balrog again and pick Fighter, Page, or Spearman[^1]. Nexon's page calls the first job Swordsman[^2].

The official notes name no requirement other than the level. If the game asks for more, please [send a correction](/contributing/) with a screenshot.

Founder's Access has first and second job only[^1]. Third job was open for testing at level 70 in Closed Online Test 2, and Nexon said test content may change[^3]. Nexon has not said when third job comes to the live game. Inkwell said content such as third job may arrive in stages[^4].

## Stats

</Fragment>

STR is the Warrior's main stat. It raises Warrior damage and Weapon Def. DEX raises Warrior damage a little and raises Accuracy[^5].

You get 5 AP each level. From level 10 you get 3 SP each level, plus 1 extra SP when you take first and second job[^5].

</Window>

<Fragment slot="title">

## Skills by job

</Fragment>

Nexon's Warrior page[^2] lists these skills. It also explains what each one does.


<SkillGrid classId="warrior" />

All three second jobs list Rush. Fighter and Page share some skill names, but the page gives them different effects. Fighter's Final Attack: Sword hits up to 3 enemies, and Page's hits one[^2].

Skill levels, costs, and damage numbers are not in the official pages yet. If you have them from the game, please [send a correction](/contributing/) with a screenshot.
