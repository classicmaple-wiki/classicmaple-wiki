At level 10, talk to [Athena Pierce](/npcs/athena-pierce/) in [Henesys](/places/henesys/) to become a Bowman[^1]. At level 30, talk to Athena Pierce again and pick Hunter or Crossbowman[^1]. Nexon's page calls the first job Archer[^2].

The official notes name no requirement other than the level. If the game asks for more, please add it with a screenshot.

Founder's Access has first and second job only[^1]. Third job was open for testing at level 70 in Closed Online Test 2, and Nexon said test content may change[^3]. Nexon has not said when third job comes to the live game. Inkwell said content such as third job may arrive in stages[^4].

## Stats

</Fragment>

DEX is the Bowman's main stat. It raises Bowman damage and Accuracy. STR raises Bowman damage a little and raises Weapon Def[^5].

You get 5 AP each level. From level 10 you get 3 SP each level, plus 1 extra SP when you take first and second job[^5].

</Window>

<Window id="skills-by-job">
<Fragment slot="title">

## Skills by job

</Fragment>

Nexon's Bowman page[^2] lists these skills. It also explains what each one does.

- **Archer (first job):** Critical Shot, The Eye of Amazon, Focus, Power Knockback, Arrow Blow, Double Shot.
- **Hunter (second job):** Bow Mastery, Amazon's Judgement, Final Attack: Bow, Bow Booster, Soul Arrow: Bow, Arrow Bomb.
- **Crossbowman (second job):** Crossbow Mastery, Amazon's Judgement, Final Attack: Crossbow, Crossbow Booster, Soul Arrow: Crossbow, Iron Arrow.

Skill levels, costs, and damage numbers are not in the official pages yet. If you have them from the game, please add them with a screenshot.
