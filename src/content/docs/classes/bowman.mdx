At level 10, talk to [Athena Pierce](/npcs/athena-pierce/) in [Henesys](/places/henesys/) to become a Bowman ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)). At level 30, talk to Athena Pierce again and pick Hunter or Crossbowman ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)). Nexon's page calls the first job Archer ([Bowman page](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/classes-and-jobs/46085/bowman)).

The official notes name no requirement other than the level. If the game asks for more, please add it with a screenshot.

Founder's Access has first and second job only ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)). Third job was open for testing at level 70 in Closed Online Test 2, and Nexon said test content may change ([test 2 notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/43432/closed-online-test-2-release-notes)). Nexon has not said when third job comes to the live game. Inkwell said content such as third job may arrive in stages ([interview](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45681)).

## Stats

</Fragment>

DEX is the Bowman's main stat. It raises Bowman damage and Accuracy. STR raises Bowman damage a little and raises Weapon Def ([Character Stats guide](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/maple-guides/all/46103/character-stats)).

You get 5 AP each level. From level 10 you get 3 SP each level, plus 1 extra SP when you take first and second job ([Character Stats guide](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/maple-guides/all/46103/character-stats)).

</Window>

## Skills by job

</Fragment>

Nexon's [Bowman page](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/classes-and-jobs/46085/bowman) lists these skills. It also explains what each one does.

- **Archer (first job):** Critical Shot, The Eye of Amazon, Focus, Power Knockback, Arrow Blow, Double Shot.
- **Hunter (second job):** Bow Mastery, Amazon's Judgement, Final Attack: Bow, Bow Booster, Soul Arrow: Bow, Arrow Bomb.
- **Crossbowman (second job):** Crossbow Mastery, Amazon's Judgement, Final Attack: Crossbow, Crossbow Booster, Soul Arrow: Crossbow, Iron Arrow.

Skill levels, costs, and damage numbers are not in the official pages yet. If you have them from the game, please add them with a screenshot.
