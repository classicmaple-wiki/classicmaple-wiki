At level 10, talk to [Dark Lord](/npcs/dark-lord/) in [Kerning City](/places/kerning-city/) to become a Thief ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)). At level 30, talk to Dark Lord again and pick Assassin or Bandit ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)). Nexon's page calls the first job Rogue ([Thief page](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/classes-and-jobs/46086/thief)).

The official notes name no requirement other than the level. If the game asks for more, please add it with a screenshot.

Founder's Access has first and second job only ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)). Third job was open for testing at level 70 in Closed Online Test 2, and Nexon said test content may change ([test 2 notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/43432/closed-online-test-2-release-notes)). Nexon has not said when third job comes to the live game. Inkwell said content such as third job may arrive in stages ([interview](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45681)).

## Stats

</Fragment>

LUK is the Thief's main stat. It raises Thief damage and Evasion, raises Accuracy a little, and cuts the EXP you lose when you die. DEX raises Thief damage a little and raises Accuracy. STR also raises Thief damage a little ([Character Stats guide](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/maple-guides/all/46103/character-stats)).

You get 5 AP each level. From level 10 you get 3 SP each level, plus 1 extra SP when you take first and second job ([Character Stats guide](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/maple-guides/all/46103/character-stats)).

## Skills by job

</Fragment>

Nexon's [Thief page](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/classes-and-jobs/46086/thief) lists these skills. It also explains what each one does.

- **Rogue (first job):** Nimble Body, Keen Eyes, Disorder, Dark Sight, Double Stab, Lucky Seven.
- **Assassin (second job):** Claw Mastery, Critical Throw, Critical Recovery, Claw Booster, Haste, Drain.
- **Bandit (second job):** Dagger Mastery, Nimble Recovery, Dagger Booster, Haste, Steal, Savage Blow.

Skill levels, costs, and damage numbers are not in the official pages yet. If you have them from the game, please add them with a screenshot.

## Announced fixes

</Fragment>

Nexon's notice for the October 15, 2026 maintenance lists these Thief fixes ([October 15 notice](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/maintenance/46523)):

- Dark Sight needs Disorder at level 3. The skill text wrongly said level 1.
- Steal no longer drops mesos or items, steals, or gives its buff when the attack misses.
- You can no longer open a Store Permit shop while Dark Sight is on. Before, other players could not see the shop.

Nexon has not announced other Thief skill changes for Classic World.
