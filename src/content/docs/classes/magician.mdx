At level 10, talk to [Grendel the Really Old](/npcs/grendel-the-really-old/) in [Ellinia](/places/ellinia/) to become a Magician[^1]. At level 30, talk to Grendel again and pick Wizard (Fire/Poison), Wizard (Ice/Lightning), or Cleric[^1].

The official notes name no requirement other than the level. If the game asks for more, please [send a correction](/contributing/) with a screenshot.

Founder's Access has first and second job only[^1]. Third job was open for testing at level 70 in Closed Online Test 2, and Nexon said test content may change[^2]. Nexon has not said when third job comes to the live game. Inkwell said content such as third job may arrive in stages[^3].

## Stats

</Fragment>

INT is the Magician's main stat. It raises Magic Attack, raises Magician damage and Accuracy a little, and raises Magic Def a little. Nexon lists LUK as the secondary stat. LUK raises Evasion, raises Accuracy a little, and cuts the EXP you lose when you die (Character Stats guide[^4], Magician page[^5]).

You get 5 AP each level. From level 10 you get 3 SP each level, plus 1 extra SP when you take first and second job[^4].

## Announced fixes

</Fragment>

The October 8, 2026 maintenance fixed two Cleric Heal bugs[^6]:

- Heal reached farther than intended.
- Heal could be cast without enough MP.

Nexon's notice for the October 15, 2026 maintenance lists these fixes[^7]:

- The game could disconnect when Poison Breath hit a monster.
- The game could disconnect when you cast Heal on party members near Zombie Mushrooms in the Ant Tunnel.
- The Magic Armor afterimage drifted away from the character.

Nexon has not announced other Magician skill changes for Classic World.
