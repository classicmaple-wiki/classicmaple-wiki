At level 10, talk to [Grendel the Really Old](/npcs/grendel-the-really-old/) in [Ellinia](/places/ellinia/) to become a Magician ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)). At level 30, talk to Grendel again and pick Wizard (Fire/Poison), Wizard (Ice/Lightning), or Cleric ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)).

The official notes name no requirement other than the level. If the game asks for more, please add it with a screenshot.

Founder's Access has first and second job only ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)). Third job was open for testing at level 70 in Closed Online Test 2, and Nexon said test content may change ([test 2 notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/43432/closed-online-test-2-release-notes)). Nexon has not said when third job comes to the live game. Inkwell said content such as third job may arrive in stages ([interview](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45681)).

## Stats

</Fragment>

INT is the Magician's main stat. It raises Magic Attack, raises Magician damage and Accuracy a little, and raises Magic Def a little. Nexon lists LUK as the secondary stat. LUK raises Evasion, raises Accuracy a little, and cuts the EXP you lose when you die ([Character Stats guide](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/maple-guides/all/46103/character-stats), [Magician page](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/classes-and-jobs/46084/magician)).

You get 5 AP each level. From level 10 you get 3 SP each level, plus 1 extra SP when you take first and second job ([Character Stats guide](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/maple-guides/all/46103/character-stats)).

## Skills by job

</Fragment>

Nexon's [Magician page](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/classes-and-jobs/46084/magician) lists these skills. It also explains what each one does.

- **Magician (first job):** Improved MP Recovery, Max MP Increase, Magic Guard, Magic Armor, Energy Bolt, Magic Claw.
- **Wizard (Fire/Poison) (second job):** MP Eater, Meditation, Teleport, Slow, Fire Arrow, Poison Breath.
- **Wizard (Ice/Lightning) (second job):** MP Eater, Meditation, Teleport, Slow, Cold Beam, Thunder Bolt.
- **Cleric (second job):** MP Eater, Teleport, Heal, Invincible, Bless, Holy Arrow.

Skill levels, costs, and damage numbers are not in the official pages yet. If you have them from the game, please add them with a screenshot.

## Announced fixes

</Fragment>

The October 8, 2026 maintenance fixed two Cleric Heal bugs ([October 8 notice](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/maintenance/46321/completed-unscheduled-maintenance-october-8-2026)):

- Heal reached farther than intended.
- Heal could be cast without enough MP.

Nexon's notice for the October 15, 2026 maintenance lists these fixes ([October 15 notice](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/maintenance/46523)):

- The game could disconnect when Poison Breath hit a monster.
- The game could disconnect when you cast Heal on party members near Zombie Mushrooms in the Ant Tunnel.
- The Magic Armor afterimage drifted away from the character.

Nexon has not announced other Magician skill changes for Classic World.
