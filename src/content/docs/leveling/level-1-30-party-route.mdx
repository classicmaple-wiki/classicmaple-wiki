## The plan

Facts below come from Nexon's official posts. Maps and level breakpoints are still needed, because Nexon has not
published a leveling route.

<Steps>

1. **Level 1 to first job.** Every character starts as a Beginner on [Maple Island](/places/maple-island/). You
   fight your first monsters and do quests there before you travel to Victoria Island
   ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)). Maple Island
   has two small towns, Amherst and Southperry. A boat from Southperry takes you to Victoria Island, and Lith Harbor
   is where many new players arrive ([world map](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/world-map)). First job
   opens at level 10 ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)).
   Maps and quests needed.

2. **First job to Kerning Party Quest.** Maps needed, with the level to move on and the reason.

3. **Kerning Party Quest.** The party quest is called First Time Together. Characters at level 21 and up can join.
   Talk to [Lakelis](/npcs/lakelis/) in [Kerning City](/places/kerning-city/) to start. Your party solves puzzles,
   fights monsters, and faces King Slime
   ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)). Since
   October 8, 2026, it runs only on channels 3, 4, 5, and 6
   ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)). Party size
   and stage notes are needed. The release notes do not give them. Confirm them in game before writing them down. Do
   not assume they match original MapleStory.

4. **After the party quest, up to 30.** Second job opens at level 30
   ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)). Maps needed.

</Steps>
