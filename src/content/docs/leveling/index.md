## What Nexon says about the early game

These facts come from Nexon's official posts. They are not a route.

- Every character starts as a Beginner on Maple Island, then travels to Victoria Island[^1].
- Maple Island has the towns of Amherst and Southperry. A boat from Southperry goes to Victoria Island[^2].
- Nexon lists three Beginner skills: Three Snails, Recovery, and Nimble Feet[^3].
- From level 1 to 9 you get 1 SP each level, for Beginner skills only. By level 10 you will have enough SP to max every Beginner skill[^4].
- You get 5 AP each level. From level 10 you get 3 SP each level, plus 1 extra SP at first and second job[^4].
- First job opens at level 10 and second job at level 30. The level cap in Founder's Access is 100[^1].
- You can stay a Beginner past level 10 and pick a job later[^3].
- You must leave Maple Island before you can open Founder's Package items[^5].
- The Kerning City Party Quest is open from level 21[^1].
- Forgotten Hollow is not open in Founder's Access. Nexon plans to add it later[^1].
- Nexon's October 15 notice fixes a bug where monsters killed by damage over time, such as burn or bleed, gave double EXP and drops[^6].

Nexon has not posted maps, monster levels, or EXP rates for leveling. Those come from players. If you have tested a
map, [send a correction](/contributing/) with a screenshot.
