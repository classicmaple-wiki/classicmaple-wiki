## What Nexon says about the early game

These facts come from Nexon's official posts. They are not a route.

- Every character starts as a Beginner on Maple Island, then travels to Victoria Island ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)).
- Maple Island has the towns of Amherst and Southperry. A boat from Southperry goes to Victoria Island ([world map](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/world-map)).
- Nexon lists three Beginner skills: Three Snails, Recovery, and Nimble Feet ([Beginner page](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/classes-and-jobs/46038/beginner)).
- From level 1 to 9 you get 1 SP each level, for Beginner skills only. By level 10 you will have enough SP to max every Beginner skill ([Character Stats guide](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/maple-guides/all/46103/character-stats)).
- You get 5 AP each level. From level 10 you get 3 SP each level, plus 1 extra SP at first and second job ([Character Stats guide](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/maple-guides/all/46103/character-stats)).
- First job opens at level 10 and second job at level 30. The level cap in Founder's Access is 100 ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)).
- You can stay a Beginner past level 10 and pick a job later ([Beginner page](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/game/mscw/classes-and-jobs/46038/beginner)).
- You must leave Maple Island before you can open Founder's Package items ([Founder's Access Has Begun](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45490/founder-s-access-has-begun)).
- The Kerning City Party Quest is open from level 21 ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)).
- Forgotten Hollow is not open in Founder's Access. Nexon plans to add it later ([release notes](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/general/45621/founder-s-access-release-notes)).
- Nexon's October 15 notice fixes a bug where monsters killed by damage over time, such as burn or bleed, gave double EXP and drops ([October 15 notice](https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/maintenance/46523)).

Nexon has not posted maps, monster levels, or EXP rates for leveling. Those come from players. If you have tested a
map, add it with a screenshot.
