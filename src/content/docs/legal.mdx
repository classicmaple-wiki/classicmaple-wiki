## Pictures

- **Sprites and minimaps** of monsters, NPCs, items, and maps are Nexon's art. We show them small, to help players identify things, and take them from maplestory.io.
- **Scene paintings and class characters** are original art made for this wiki with AI image tools and edited by hand.

## What you send us

Corrections sent through the form or GitHub are published on this site under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Only send screenshots you took yourself. If you leave a name, we may credit you by that name. See [How to contribute](/contributing/).

## No warranty

Everything here is provided as is, with no warranty. Values change between game builds. Check in game when it matters.

## Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Details are on the [privacy page](/privacy/).

## Removal requests

If you own something on this site and want it removed or credited differently, <a href={`${repositoryUrl}/issues/new?template=removal-request.yml`}>open a removal request</a>. We act on these within a few days.
