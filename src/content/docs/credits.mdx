## MapleClassic Wiki

Part of our data comes from [MapleClassic Wiki](https://mapleclassic.wiki/), by MapleClassic Wiki contributors, licensed [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).

- **What we took:** names, where monsters, NPCs, and shops are, who gives a quest, which quests come first, what shops sell, monster drops that players observed, and core numbers: monster level, HP, and EXP, item required level, and map level ranges. Those numbers are from the closed beta and may differ at launch.
- **What we left out:** item stats, prices, rewards, descriptions, dialogue, other text, images, and the names of the players who reported drops.
- **What we changed:** we extracted the facts and reformatted them into our own pages.
- **Who wrote it:** every imported page links its source page and that page's history, where the editors are named. Drop reports come from MapleClassic Wiki players and editors, whose names are kept there.

Classic Maple Wiki is independent of MapleClassic Wiki and is not endorsed by it or its editors. Their [General disclaimer](https://mapleclassic.wiki/wiki/MapleWiki:General_disclaimer) applies to their content.

## Drop rates

Drop rates are community estimates from player kill counts, not official numbers. Each one shows how many kills it is based on and when it was observed. Small samples can be far off.
